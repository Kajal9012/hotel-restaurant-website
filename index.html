About Us

Hotel & Restaurant

Welcome to Sanjivani Hotel & Restaurant, where comfort, quality, and taste come together to create a memorable experience. We offer our guests a clean, peaceful, and comfortable stay with modern facilities designed to make you feel at home. Whether you are traveling with family or visiting for business, our hotel provides the perfect environment for relaxation and convenience. Our restaurant serves delicious, hygienic, and freshly prepared food, inspired by authentic Indian flavors. From family dinners to special occasions, we aim to make every meal enjoyable and satisfying. At Sanjivani Hotel & Restaurant, customer satisfaction is our top priority. Our friendly and professional staff is always ready to provide warm hospitality and excellent service. Come and experience comfort, care, and great taste — only at Sanjivani Hotel & Restaurant.
